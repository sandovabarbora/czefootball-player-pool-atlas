This autumn

Atlas data as of 14 September 2026; squad news as of 29 September 2026

Since the World Cup the national team has a new head coach, and three players of the pool have retired from it. The dated facts below come from the sources listed at the end; every number is the atlas’s own, from its snapshot, which this section does not refresh.

A new head coach

Miroslav Koubek left the post of head coach by mutual agreement on 29 June 2026.1 Santi Denia was appointed head coach on 31 July 2026, on a two-year contract,2 and presented publicly on 6 August 2026.3

Three retirements from the national team

Each of the three announced his own retirement from international football before Santi Denia was appointed; none of them was left out by a selection decision.4
